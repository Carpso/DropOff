### DropOff rider fee guidance (Lusaka pilot)

- Standard city pickup: **K35**.
- Optional destination door delivery: **K30**.
- Platform take-rate target: **20%** of the rider fee.
- Courier fare is always separate and shown as “quote needed” until a verified rate exists.

**DropOff rider fee guidance (Lusaka pilot)**
- Zone 1 (within Lusaka): **K25–45** flat per collect-and-drop (5 km radius).
- Zone 1.5 (north / south Lusaka): K60–80.
- Banded by distance (haversine from the depot), not by weight — the weight is already billed by the courier.
- **Cash-handling surcharge** (if a rider advances courier fees at the counter): +5% capping.
