# Cover is “quoted protection” — NOT insurance (internal draft)

> Draft. Not legal advice. Do not rely on this without counsel.

## What DropOff cover is today
- An optional **quoted protection** add-on recorded on a job: declared value, cover fee, cover status.
- It is **not** a regulated insurance product: there is no underwriter, no policy document, no claims process, no reserving, no licence.

## What must be in the UI / terms
Every screen that mentions cover must say:
- “Quoted protection, not insurance.”
- “Limits apply. Evidence required (photo/waybill).”
- “Not guaranteed; subject to review.”
- “No claim payout exists until a binding cover agreement is signed.”

## What must NOT be said
- “Insured”, “Guaranteed”, “Protected”, “Assured”, “Assessed” — implies insurance.
- “Covered if lost/damaged” without a defined limit, evidence checklist and resolver.
- “Refund guaranteed”.

## What it takes to become real insurance
1. Underwriter + licence (regulated entity in Zambia) or a binding partner agreement.
2. Premium collection, claims adjudication, reserving, PCI/CCPA obligations.
3. Terms: exclusions, evidence, limits, subrogation, dispute window.
4. Full transparency in-app + terms + privacy.

## Product today
- Good: records declared value/cover status (`covered`, `quoted/declined/none`).
- Needed: clear disclaimers, no implied insurance claims, no payouts promised.
- Recommended: keep “None / Declined / Quoted” only. No payment outcome for loss/damage until insurance/legal sign-off.
