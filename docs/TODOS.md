## Payment positioning — prepaid parcel delivery, never a wallet (2026-11-10)
- [done/server-done] **Five compliance guards (owner directive + regulation review):** DropOff prepayments are a single-purpose *prepayment for parcel delivery* — (1) every prepayment is pinned to one parcel via `booking_deposit` → `job_id`; (2) **no cash-out, ever** — refunds return to the sender's source momo only (`lipilaDisburse`), manually via `/admin/payments/:id/refund` or automatically on cancel-before-pickup (`refundUnspentPrepaid` in the no-show cron, new worker `6023279b`); (3) cancel-before-pickup **auto-refunds** the unspent prepayment (event `prepaid_refund_failed` flags payout failures for manual refund); (4) **no free-form customer top-up exists** — `/payments` `kind='topup'` is rider/ops COD float only, gated and capped at `WALLET_TOPUP_CAP_ZMW`; the customer prompt is the server-computed quote (trip price + processing + momo leg) and can never exceed the delivery cost — balances are residue of one booking, not a wallet; (5) **never accepted by third parties** (not depots/counters/riders/merchants/P2P) — "wallet" wording removed from all customer-facing copy; SMS now says "prepayment for your parcel delivery (trip price + processing fee)… returned to your momo". Legislation basis: National Payment System Act 2026 (No. 5 of 2026) + Banking & Financial Services Act 2026 + BoZ E-Money Directives 2023 (Gazette 958/2023) — e-money = store of value **accepted by others + redeemable on demand**; DropOff meets neither. Full standard: `docs/PAYMENT_COMPLIANCE.md`. Riders hold zero cash; plans are prepaid subscriptions. **Follow-up:** on first real Lipila payout test the end-to-end cancel-refund; get a written legal opinion before any top-up-like expansion.
