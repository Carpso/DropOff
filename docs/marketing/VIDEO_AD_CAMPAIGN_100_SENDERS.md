**Brand:** DropOff (a Carpso Solutions product)
**Slogan:** WE CARRY IT TO THE COURIER
**Support / WhatsApp:** 0968551110 (+260 968 551 110)
**Markets:** Lusaka first, then Ndola / Kitwe
**Goal:** 100 verified sender signups + first booking in 14 days

## 1. The maths before the creative

Do not shoot anything until the funnel maths is agreed. 100 signups is a *funnel* target, not a video target.

| Stage | Assumed rate | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Video views (3s+) | — | 60,000 |
| View → profile/landing click | 2.5% | 1,500 |
| Click → WhatsApp message | 45% | 675 |
| WhatsApp → install | 55% | 371 |
| Install → signup (OTP done) | 60% | 223 |
| Signup → first booking | 45% | **100** |

So: **~60k views, ~675 WhatsApp conversations, ~370 installs.** At Zambian CPMs that is roughly K1,500–K3,500 of paid boost if you also use organic (Status, TikTok, rider referrals). Organic alone can do it if every rider posts daily — see §12.

**Budget-split rule:** 60% paid video, 25% WhatsApp/click-to-chat ads, 15% data bundles for the 3 riders who post daily.

## 2. Non-negotiable truth rules (read before writing any line)

These exist because a false claim becomes a support ticket, a refund, or a regulator problem.

1. **Never say "insured".** Cover is *quoted protection*, priced per parcel, confirmed at booking. Say "you can declare a value and add protection" — never "we insure".
2. **Never publish a courier fare.** Courier prices are *quoted at the counter*. Say "you see the depot fare on the photographed receipt before you repay it".
3. **Never say DropOff collects the courier money.** The rider fronts the depot fare; the sender repays the *exact photographed receipt* through DropOff. It is a **reimbursement**, not a DropOff charge.
4. **Never show K15 as a fee.** The depot queue fee is **waived** — say "no depot queue fee".
5. **Never publish 0976847775.** That is the transaction line. Users only ever see **0968551110**.
6. **Plans are introductory promo pricing.** If you name a price on camera, the same frame must carry "promo pricing — rises within 3–6 months, your current cycle keeps its rate".
7. **Delivery windows are real:** 05:30–07:30, 10:00–11:30, 14:00–15:30. Never say "any time" or "in 30 minutes".
8. **Same-city recipients get no PIN.** They confirm receipt in-app. Do not film a fake 4-digit door code for a Lusaka→Lusaka job.

## 5. The offer that gets the 100

Pick **one** and put it on the end card. Do not stack offers.

**Option A — "First 100" (recommended, cheapest, fully controllable)**
> **First 100 senders get a Daily Rider pass free — 1 parcel, today, no rider fee.**
> Claim inside the app with code **SEND100**.

Cost: K49 × 100 = **K4,900** of rider-fee value (you still collect processing fee, so net cost is lower). Mechanic: ops mints 100 one-time **pack codes** for `rider_daily` and hands one to each verified signup in WhatsApp. No new code is needed — pack-code redemption already exists.

**Option B — Referral-only**
> **Send a friend. When their first delivery completes and is paid, you both get rewarded.**

Mechanic: existing `FRIEND` referral codes; reward issues only after the referee's first *paid, delivered* job (already enforced server-side). Zero upfront cost, slower ramp.

**Option C — Bundle**
Daily Rider free for the first 100 **and** referral rewards after that. Use this if you want both the spike and the compounding.

## 8. AI video generation prompts

Use these with Veo / Sora / Runway / Kling / Pika. Generate **shot by shot**, 5–8 seconds each, then edit to the table in §4. Do not try to generate the whole ad in one prompt — consistency collapses.

### 8.0 STYLE BIBLE (paste this into every prompt)

> **Master style:** photorealistic cinematic commercial, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural African daylight, warm golden highlights, deep forest-green accents (brand colour) and amber highlights, subtle film grain, 24fps, handheld-stabilised camera with slight drift, no lens flare, no text, no watermark. Contemporary Lusaka, Zambia. Real-looking people, authentic clothing, natural skin texture, no plastic skin, no uncanny faces.

### 8.1 Negative prompt (every shot)

> no text, no captions, no logos generated by the model, no watermark, no extra fingers, no deformed hands, no morphing faces, no floating objects, no western/european streets, no snow, no fast cuts, no strobe, no cartoon, no 3D render look, no oversaturation, no stock-photo smiles

### 8.1 Shot prompts

**S1 — Closed sign (0:00)**
> Medium close-up, 35mm, a Zambian woman in her early 30s wearing a smart wax-print blouse flips a small wooden "CLOSED" sign toward the camera inside a small boutique. Shelves of fabric and boxes behind her. Two blurred customers turn away in the background doorway, out of focus. Her expression is tired frustration, jaw tight, eyes down. ### 8.3 Voiceover direction

- Male or female, Zambian accent, 30–40, **conversational not announcer**.
- Pace: 3.5 words/second for hooks, 3.0 for body. The 45s script is ~120 words — that is the ceiling.
- Hard consonants on "courier", "counter", "receipt". Never shout the brand.
- Record 3 takes: warm, urgent, amused. Test warm first; urgent converts better in the 3s hook only.

### 8.4 Music & SFX

- 92–100 BPM, afro-pop / amapiano-adjacent but instrumental (no vocals competing with VO).
- Beat drop lands at **0:03** (end of hook) and again at **0:41** (logo).
- SFX: door slam, 4 × shutter click, counter bell, single unlock click, one horn. Nothing else.

## 11. Funnel wiring (this is what actually produces the 100)

1. **Every ad CTA goes to WhatsApp, not the store.** `https://wa.me/260968551110?text=SEND100` — the prefilled text is your attribution.
2. **Use 4 different prefilled texts** so you know which creative paid: `SEND100-TT`, `SEND100-FB`, `SEND100-STATUS`, `SEND100-FLYER`.
3. **WhatsApp reply #1 is instant and contains the install link + code.** Do not make them ask. (See the WhatsApp guide.)
4. **Code gates the free parcel.** Sender installs → signs in with OTP → sends you their number → you issue one `rider_daily` pack code → they redeem in Account → Plans.
5. **Log every sender in a sheet:** number, date joined, code issued, first booking date, first delivery date, source tag.
6. **Follow up on day 2 and day 5** if no booking: "Your free parcel is still waiting — when should the rider come?" That single message typically converts 20–30%.

**Tracking sheet columns:** `phone | name | source_tag | code_issued | first_booking_at | first_delivery_at | plan_purchased | repaid_counter_fare | notes`

## 14. What NOT to film

- No fake queues at a courier counter with actors in courier-branded shirts (trademark + defamation risk).
- No competitor logos, no naming a competitor.
- No "guaranteed next-day" claims.
- No shots of a blurred-then-paid receipt that implies DropOff charged the depot fare. The VO must say **"repay"**.
- No riders holding cash on camera (reads as informal money handling).
- No customer faces without a signed release.
