## How booking works
You choose a depot/courier and destination, describe the parcel, and pay the **DropOff fee** for the rider leg (plus one processing fee). The courier's own parcel fare is set and collected by your nominated courier at their depot counter — it is never charged through DropOff. The server sets the final DropOff price; estimates shown in the app are not binding until confirmed at booking. Riders carry parcels to the destination depot, where the recipient collects them. Sellers on a monthly pack (Silver / Gold / Platinum) have the rider leg bundled inside the pack price for parcels within the pack's monthly cap.

## Payments
Payment is handled by Lipila mobile money. You pay when you book or on delivery where cash is accepted. Failed payments cancel the booking automatically. Refunds, when due, go to the same mobile-money number.
