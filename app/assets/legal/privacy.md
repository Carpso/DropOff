# DropOff — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

## Who we are
DropOff is Zambia’s first-mile parcel marketplace: senders book riders to carry parcels between cities and depots, and pay through Lipila mobile money. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights over it.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** email address, full name, and mobile-money phone number (used for refunds, payouts, and job status).
- **Job details:** pickup/drop-off locations and cities, recipient name and phone, parcel description, weight class, payment method, and booking history.
- **Location:** your live GPS fix only when you explicitly share it through the Rider live position feature; it is shown to the sender of your jobs so they can track the parcel.
- **Device & push:** your Firebase Cloud Messaging token and device platform so we can send you job status and payment notifications. You can turn this off by signing out.
- **Payments:** Lipila transaction references and payment status. We never see or store mobile-money PINs or card numbers.

## Why we collect it
- To match you with riders and couriers, complete bookings, and route parcels.
- To pay riders and send refunds via mobile money.
- To show you your booking history and standing orders.
- To send push notifications about your parcels and payments.
- To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

## Sharing
- Your pickup/drop-off details go only to the rider handling your job and the depot on the route.
- Payment processing goes through Lipila under Lipila’s own terms.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights
You may ask to see, correct, or delete your account data at any time by emailing support or using the sign-out/unlink flow, which removes your stored session and push token from this device. Server records needed for financial audit are kept as required by law.

## Security
API calls are JWT-authenticated over HTTPS. Sessions can be revoked with a password reset, and push tokens are keyed to your phone so another user on the same device cannot receive your notifications.
