## One-paragraph pitch

DropOff is a Zambian delivery app: a sender pins their exact pickup location, a
salaried rider collects every item with a time-stamped camera photo, and the
item is tracked live on our own self-hosted map through to the chosen courier
depot or recipient door. We have shipped v0.6.1 of the Android app (offline
map, turn-by-turn navigator, accept/decline order curtain, MFA, full staff
console). **We have zero users today** — that is why this is a $5k pilot, not a
funding round. The money buys four licences, four used Suzuki bikes, a first
month of salaries and the marketing push to reach 100 repeat senders in 90 days.

## The ask

- **Amount:** $5,000 (~K130,000 at ~26 ZMW/USD — verify the live rate).
- **Structure:** pilot sponsorship / grant, no equity. Fortnightly metrics note
  + sponsor logo on depot flyers. If the investor prefers equity, offer a
  simple SAFE and let them review terms — never offer both in one sentence.
- **Use of funds (estimates; replace bike prices with 3 real quotes):**

| Item | K |
|---|---:|
| 4 rider licences × K1,500 | 6,000 |
| 4 used Suzuki 100–150cc × ~K9,000 *(quote needed)* | 36,000 |
| Helmets, phone mounts, hi-vis, spares | 6,000 |
| First-month salaries, 4 × K3,000 | 12,000 |
| Fuel + airtime, month 1 | 8,000 |
| Marketing + user acquisition | 40,000 |
| 100 sender promo credits × K100 | 10,000 |
| Paperwork + contingency | 12,000 |
| **Total** | **K130,000** |

New bikes (~K16–20k each) do not fit this budget — buy used, license first.

## Revenue model (say it exactly this way)

Income is **sender subscriptions and prepaid platform fees**. Riders are
salaried so they can guarantee the 06:00 / 10:00 / 14:00 depot slots. DropOff
does **not** collect depot/courier fares — those are paid at the counter. Don't
pitch "cash from other rides" unless that revenue line is formally agreed.

## 90-day milestones

- **Week 1:** 4 licences issued, 4 used bikes bought, riders trained.
- **Week 2:** 25 senders with a first completed booking.
- **Week 3:** 50 senders; first 10 repeat bookings.
- **Week 4:** 100 senders with ≥2 completed bookings ("recurring" = repeat).
- **Weeks 5–12:** retain those senders on plans; publish fortnightly metrics.

## Safety rules

- Never send API keys, keystore, admin keys or internal D1 data in a DM.
- Verify the person with a call before any money moves.
- Reject any "send a processing fee first" request — that is a scam.
- Use one structure (sponsorship **or** SAFE), and put it in a simple written agreement.
- These are draft templates, not legal or financial advice.
