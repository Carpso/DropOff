# Brand style guide — DropOff print & social

**Palette**
- Forest ink `#10312A` — backgrounds, body on light
- Deep green `#17855F` — primary brand green, CTA buttons
- Amber `#F6B73C` — the highlight (badges, codes, stamps, stars)
- Cream `#FFF8EC` — paper background
- Paper beige `#F4EDE0` — secondary panels
- Text on cream: `#10312A`; on forest: `#FFF8EC`; accents amber `#F6B73C` only on dark

**Typeface**
- Headline: heavy sans, uppercase or tight-tracked all-caps where possible (Archivo Black / Impact-like / Helvetica Bold condensed)
- Body: same family regular
- Max 2 sizes per layout step (display + small)
- Never serif. Never light weight for main copy.

**Rules**
- One idea per surface. One CTA per asset.
- Never quote courier fares we haven't verified or been given in writing.
- Never imply DropOff collects partner money in-app (it's "coming soon" honestly tagged).
- Every flyer carries: codebox (FIRSTM10/SELLER20), wa.me CTA, hours or timeframe of the sweep.
- Stamped / verified motif: circle, dashed border, amber; stays under 15% of the canvas.
- Logo: wordmark "DROPOFF" letterspaced + pickup arrow motif. Never redraw; use asset from `assets/brand/`.
