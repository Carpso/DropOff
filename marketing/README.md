# DropOff — Marketing Kit (v1, Nov 2026)

**One-liner:** We carry it to the courier.

**What DropOff is:** first-mile errand-rider from any Lusaka home/shop/online seller's door to their courier of choice (Platinum, Zampost, PostDotNet, Icypeed, Mercury, Rovert, One World, …). Book in the app; a rider sweeps it same day or next; tracking ends with verified proof-of-drop at the depot counter.

**Who we sell to (in order of urgency):**
1. **Online sellers** (Instagram/Facebook/Whatsapp shops) — biggest pain: parcels sitting unshipped = "money sitting".
2. **E-commerce sellers** moving stock to Copperbelt/Livingstone weekly.
3. **Corporate despatch desks** needing same-day courier runs.

**What we never claim:** intercity courier replacement; partner payment collection (in-app partner pay is "coming soon", honest with partners & sellers).
