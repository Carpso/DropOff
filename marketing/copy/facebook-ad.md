# Facebook ad — launch brief (Sellers, Nov–Dec 2026)

**Objective:** Conversions (WhatsApp click-to-chat) or Messages.
**Campaign name:** "DropOff-Seller-Accel-01"

**Audience (Lusaka, 22–45):**
- Location: Lusaka province (include Kafue, Chongwe radius K100)
- Interests: online selling, e-commerce, Facebook seller, small business, delivery, logistics, digital payments, mobile money, home-based business, Instagram business
- Behavior: small business owners, online purchasers, mobile device users
- Exclusions: courier companies / logistics company employees (job titles: courier, delivery driver, logistics manager)

**Placements:** Facebook Feed + IG Feed + Messenger + WA (start Advantage+ placement but keep it ON; monitor cost/lead).

**Budget:** K60/day days 1–3 test; scale to K120/day if cost/conversation ≦ K12; cap K2,000 total for the first 2 weeks.

**Copy variants (test A/B/C — split 33/33/33):**

**A — Money sitting**
“Your orders are not moving — they're sitting.
Book DropOff and we run your parcel to the courier of your choice — home pickup, same-day sweep.
→ First booking: **SELLER20** (20% off your DropOff charge).”
CTA: Send message via WhatsApp.

**B — Proof**
“You sell. We deliver to the depot. That's it.
Pickup at your door → counter drop-off → stamped receipt in the app.
→ **SELLER20**: first 500 bookings only."
CTA: Send message.

**C — Pack math**
“3 parcels a week = ~K450 rider fees paygo…
or Gold pack, K700/mo, 3 pickups a day, K0 rider fees, one guaranteed daily sweep.
DM ‘PACK’ — sellers keep the margin."
CTA: Learn more (WA deep link with prefilled text "I want to know about the packs").

**Creative guidance:**
- Video 15s: seller at her table packing; phone dings; rider arrives; stamp close-up; map animation arrow home→depot; end card with logo + SELLER20.
- Static: cream background, big words "YOUR ORDER IS NOT MOVING" / sub "We carry it to the courier." (A5 flyer artworks work), DropOff logo, wa.me QR.
- Caption length: keep ≤ 125 characters before the fold.

**Instant lead form (if Leads objective instead of WA):**
Name / WhatsApp number / "I ship ___ parcels per week (1 / 2–5 / 6+)" / neighborhood.
Route instant answers to WA immediately (speed to lead < 5 min wins).

**Measurement:**
- Week 1: messages, replies/no-replies, % replied with code.
- Week 2+: count first bookings (job created) vs messages (ask "how did you hear?" — code sells it).
- Kill placements with K20+ per conversation after 48 h with no conversion.
- Retarget: anyone who messaged but didn't book → K30/day, creative: "Still thinking? SELLER20 ends soon." — WA retargeting via saved audience (messenger contacts).

**Script for WA auto-reply (queued reply):**
"Hi 👋 you ordered a pickup from DropOff? Reply with: 1. Street address 2. Courier of choice (Platinum/Zampost/etc) 3. Number of parcels + sizes.\nOr install: [Play Store link]. First booking with code SELLER20."
